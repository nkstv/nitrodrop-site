What Is a Bonus Hunt?

One of the most popular formats in casino streaming, a bonus hunt turns single bonus buys into a whole event — collect first, open later, tally at the end.

How it works, in order

  1. Collect. The streamer buys into bonus rounds across a long list of different slots — sometimes dozens — without opening any of them yet. Each one just sits there, unopened, added to a running list.
  2. Tally the cost. Once collecting is done (a set number of bonuses, or a spending cap), the total amount spent gathering them all is shown — this is the number every opened bonus needs to beat.
  3. Open everything, back to back. Each collected bonus round gets played out one after another, usually smallest-bet to biggest, building toward the ones expected to pay the most.
  4. Final result. The total payout across every opened bonus is compared to what it cost to collect them all — sometimes a big profit, sometimes a loss, shown either way.

Why it works so well as a format

A bonus hunt turns a string of individually-random events into a structured story with a clear ending — will the total beat the buy-in or not? That built-in suspense is a big part of why the format shows up constantly across casino streaming, regardless of which specific streamer or casino is involved.

What to actually watch for

The running tally is the whole story — not any single bonus round. A hunt can have several huge individual hits and still finish below the collection cost if the smaller bonuses underperform, and the reverse is just as true. Judge the hunt by its final number, not its best moment.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a bonus hunt and a bonus buy?

A bonus buy is a single purchase into one bonus round. A bonus hunt is a whole session built around collecting many unopened bonus rounds across different slots first, then opening them all back to back afterward, usually with a running tally.

How do streamers decide when a bonus hunt is "full"?

Usually a set number of bonus rounds decided in advance, or a total spending cap for the collecting phase. Once that's hit, the streamer stops buying and moves into opening everything they've collected.

Does a bonus hunt guarantee a profit?

No. The total payout across every opened bonus can land above or below what was spent collecting them — that's exactly why the tally is shown live, and why some hunts end in a loss despite big individual moments along the way.
