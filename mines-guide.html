How Does Mines Work?

Mines is one of the simplest casino games to learn but one of the easiest to get wrong if you don't understand how the multiplier actually grows. Here's the short version, no fluff.

The rules, in three steps

  1. Pick your grid size and mine count. You're choosing how many hidden bombs are scattered across the board before you reveal a single tile.
  2. Reveal tiles one at a time. Every safe tile you uncover bumps your multiplier up. Hit a mine, and the round ends — you lose the bet.
  3. Cash out whenever you want. You don't have to clear the board. You can stop after one safe tile or push your luck for ten — the choice resets every round.

How the multiplier actually grows

The multiplier isn't a flat rate — it climbs faster the more mines you've chosen and the further you get into the board. That's because each safe tile you reveal is proportionally riskier than the last: fewer safe tiles remain, so uncovering one more is a bigger accomplishment, and the payout reflects that. Set 3 mines and the climb is gentle. Set 20 mines and it jumps hard with every single click.

A simple way to think about strategy

There's no formula that guarantees a win — it's a game of chance — but there is a real trade-off worth understanding before you set your mine count:

  • Low mine count (1–5): Safer, slower multiplier growth. Good if you want more rounds where you at least break even or cash out small and often.
  • High mine count (10+): Each click is a real gamble, but the multiplier can spike fast if you're willing to stop early.
  • Cash out discipline matters more than mine count. Deciding in advance "I'll stop at X multiplier" and actually sticking to it is the single biggest factor in how a session feels, win or lose.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mines rigged?

Each round, the mine positions are generated randomly the moment you place your bet — they aren't decided based on how you play afterward. The game doesn't watch your choices and adjust the odds mid-round; the layout is fixed before you click your first tile.

What's the best number of mines to pick?

There's no single correct answer — it's a trade-off. Fewer mines means safer picks and a slower-growing multiplier; more mines means each click is riskier but the multiplier climbs much faster. Pick based on how much risk you're comfortable with, not a formula.

Can you win every round of Mines?

No — Mines is a game of chance. You can improve your experience by cashing out at a point you're comfortable with, but no strategy guarantees a win on any individual round.
